## 2. Principles

This privacy policy covers the static websites www.jakob-r.de, tools.jakob-r.de and playground.jakob-r.de.

They use no cookies, no analytics or tracking services and show no advertising. All fonts, scripts and images are served from the respective website itself; no content is loaded from third-party servers, except the optional map tiles described in section 4. The search function runs entirely in your browser. Anything you enter into a tool or piece (text, files, images, camera or microphone input) is processed entirely in your browser and is never uploaded.

games.jakob-r.de has a backend and its own [privacy policy](https://games.jakob-r.de/privacy).

## 3. Hosting by GitHub Pages

www.jakob-r.de, tools.jakob-r.de and playground.jakob-r.de are hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

When you visit the website, GitHub processes technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, date and time of the request, the requested URL, referrer and browser identifier (user agent). According to GitHub, the IP addresses of visitors are logged and stored for security purposes. I have no access to this data and do not combine it with other data.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. My legitimate interest is the secure and reliable delivery of these websites. Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission has recognised as providing an adequate level of protection (Art. 45 GDPR). Further information can be found in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## 4. Map tiles in the GPX Cleaner (optional)

The GPX Cleaner at tools.jakob-r.de can show a map underneath your track. This is off by default. Only if you switch it on, your browser loads map tiles directly from the OpenStreetMap Foundation, St John's Innovation Centre, Cowley Road, Cambridge, CB4 0WS, United Kingdom. The OpenStreetMap Foundation then receives your IP address, your browser identifier and the coordinates of the requested tiles, which reveal the area your track covers. Your GPX file itself is not transmitted.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give by switching the map on and can withdraw at any time by switching it off. The European Commission has recognised the United Kingdom as providing an adequate level of protection (Art. 45 GDPR). Further information can be found in the [privacy policy of the OpenStreetMap Foundation](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).

If you contact me by email, your email address and the content of your message are processed to answer your request. The data is deleted once it is no longer needed for this purpose, unless statutory retention obligations apply.
